The Dirichlet process is the basic conjugate prior on probability measures. Its finite-dimensional Dirichlet distributions and Pólya urn prediction rule are two faces of the same object.

Finite-dimensional definition

A Dirichlet process is characterized by its masses on finite partitions.

Definition. A random probability measure \(P\) has distribution \(\operatorname{DP}(\alpha G_0)\) if for every finite measurable partition \(A_1,\ldots,A_k\), \[ (P(A_1),\ldots,P(A_k))\sim\operatorname{Dirichlet}(\alpha G_0(A_1),\ldots,\alpha G_0(A_k)). \]

Proposition. \(\mathbb E P(A)=G_0(A)\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(P(A))=G_0(A)(1-G_0(A))/(\alpha+1)\).

Proof. Use the mean and variance formulas for a beta random variable, since \(P(A)\sim\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha G_0(A),\alpha(1-G_0(A)))\).

Conjugacy

Observations update the base measure by adding point masses.

Theorem. If \(P\sim\operatorname{DP}(\alpha G_0)\) and \(X_1,\ldots,X_n\mid P\stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}P\), then \[ P\mid X_{1:n}\sim\operatorname{DP}\left(\alpha G_0+\sum_{i=1}^n\delta_{X_i}\right). \]

Proof. For any finite partition, the likelihood contributes multinomial counts to the cell probabilities. Dirichlet-multinomial conjugacy updates each partition parameter by its count. Since this holds for all finite partitions, the posterior process is the displayed Dirichlet process.

Pólya urn prediction

The predictive law is a mixture of the base distribution and the empirical distribution.

Corollary. The posterior predictive distribution is \[ X_{n+1}\mid X_{1:n}\sim \frac{\alpha}{\alpha+n}G_0+\frac1{\alpha+n}\sum_{i=1}^n\delta_{X_i}. \]

Proof. The predictive probability of \(A\) is \(\mathbb E\{P(A)\mid X_{1:n}\}\). Apply the posterior mean formula for the updated Dirichlet process.

Clustering interpretation

Repeated values occur with positive probability because the predictive distribution puts mass on previous observations.

Remark. This clustering property is the basis of Chinese restaurant process representations and Dirichlet process mixture models.

Finite-dimensional construction

Definition. A random probability measure \[ P\sim\operatorname{DP}(\alpha G_0) \] if, for every measurable partition \((A_1,\ldots,A_k)\), \[ \{P(A_1),\ldots,P(A_k)\} \sim\operatorname{Dirichlet} \{\alpha G_0(A_1),\ldots,\alpha G_0(A_k)\}. \] Here \(G_0\) is the mean distribution and \(\alpha>0\) is the concentration parameter.

Proposition. For measurable \(A,B\), \[ E\{P(A)\}=G_0(A), \] \[ \operatorname{Var}\{P(A)\} =\frac{G_0(A)\{1-G_0(A)\}}{\alpha+1}, \] \[ \operatorname{Cov}\{P(A),P(B)\} =\frac{G_0(A\cap B)-G_0(A)G_0(B)} {\alpha+1}. \]

Proof. Refine \(A,B\) to the partition \[ A\cap B,\quad A\setminus B,\quad B\setminus A,\quad(A\cup B)^c \] and apply the standard Dirichlet moment formulas. Add the appropriate cells.

Conjugate posterior process

Theorem. If \[ P\sim\operatorname{DP}(\alpha G_0), \qquad X_1,\ldots,X_n\mid P\stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}P, \] then \[ P\mid X_{1:n} \sim \operatorname{DP}\left( \alpha G_0+\sum_{i=1}^n\delta_{X_i} \right). \]

Proof. For any finite partition, observed cell counts have a multinomial likelihood. Dirichlet-multinomial conjugacy adds each count to the corresponding Dirichlet parameter. Since this holds consistently for every partition, the posterior random measure is the stated Dirichlet process.

Corollary. The posterior mean is \[ E(P\mid X_{1:n}) =\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+n}G_0 +\frac{n}{\alpha+n}\mathbb P_n. \] Thus \(\alpha\) acts as a prior precision relative to the empirical measure.

Blackwell-MacQueen prediction

Theorem. Integrating out \(P\), \[ X_{n+1}\mid X_{1:n} \sim \frac{\alpha}{\alpha+n}G_0 +\frac1{\alpha+n}\sum_{i=1}^n\delta_{X_i}. \]

Proof. \[ P(X_{n+1}\in A\mid X_{1:n}) =E\{P(A)\mid X_{1:n}\}. \] Insert the posterior mean from the corollary.

If the distinct observed values are \(\theta_1^\star,\ldots,\theta_{K_n}^\star\) with multiplicities \(n_1,\ldots,n_{K_n}\), this becomes \[ \frac{\alpha}{\alpha+n}G_0 +\sum_{k=1}^{K_n}\frac{n_k}{\alpha+n} \delta_{\theta_k^\star}. \] Existing clusters attract proportionally to size, while a new cluster is drawn from \(G_0\).

Expected number of clusters

Proposition. If \(G_0\) is nonatomic and \(K_n\) is the number of distinct values among \(X_1,\ldots,X_n\), then \[ E(K_n) =\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+i-1} =\alpha\log n+O(1). \]

Proof. Let \(I_i\) indicate that observation \(i\) starts a new cluster. The predictive rule gives \[ P(I_i=1)=\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+i-1}. \] Since \(K_n=\sum_iI_i\), linearity gives the sum. Comparison with the harmonic series gives the asymptotic expression.

Stick-breaking representation

Theorem (Sethuraman). Let \[ V_h\stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim} \operatorname{Beta}(1,\alpha), \qquad \theta_h\stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}G_0, \] independently, and define \[ w_1=V_1,\qquad w_h=V_h\prod_{\ell<h}(1-V_\ell). \] Then \[ P=\sum_{h=1}^\infty w_h\delta_{\theta_h} \sim\operatorname{DP}(\alpha G_0). \]

Proof outline. Since \[ E\{-\log(1-V_h)\}=1/\alpha>0, \] the remaining stick \(\prod_{\ell\le h}(1-V_\ell)\) tends to zero almost surely, so weights sum to one. For every partition, independently label atoms by their \(G_0\) cell. Beta-gamma algebra shows that the sums of weights in the cells have the required Dirichlet law. The finite-dimensional characterization then identifies the process.

Dirichlet process mixtures

Definition. A DP mixture is \[ Y_i\mid\theta_i\sim K(\cdot\mid\theta_i), \qquad \theta_i\mid P\sim P, \qquad P\sim\operatorname{DP}(\alpha G_0). \] After integrating \(P\), latent parameters follow the Pólya-urn rule, producing a random mixture \[ f_P(y)=\sum_hw_hK(y\mid\theta_h). \]

Clusters are not automatically components of truth. A DP mixture can estimate a smooth density consistently while the posterior number of occupied clusters fails to consistently estimate a finite true number of mixture components. Clustering is a prior-induced latent representation; interpreting each occupied cluster as a scientifically real subpopulation requires additional model and identifiability arguments.

Concentration-parameter learning

A gamma hyperprior on \(\alpha\) allows data-informed clustering. Its posterior is not conjugate in one step because the partition probability contains \[ \frac{\alpha^{K_n}\Gamma(\alpha)} {\Gamma(\alpha+n)} \prod_{k=1}^{K_n}\Gamma(n_k). \] Auxiliary-variable updates or numerical sampling are used. Large \(\alpha\) favors more, smaller clusters and makes \(P\) concentrate near \(G_0\); small \(\alpha\) favors fewer, larger clusters and greater random discreteness.
