The final page records the interface with statistics without turning numerical analysis into a statistics note.

Computation-aware inference

Definition. Computation-aware inference treats the reported estimator, posterior summary, or test statistic as the output of an approximate algorithm. Its error has statistical components and numerical components.

Likelihood computation

Proposition. Numerical likelihoods may involve quadrature, ODE solvers, matrix factorizations, or iterative optimization. If the numerical tolerance is larger than the statistical uncertainty, inference can be dominated by computation rather than sampling.

Monte Carlo versus deterministic error

Definition. Monte Carlo error is stochastic and usually scales like \(n^{-1/2}\) under independence. Deterministic discretization error often scales like \(h^p\). Hybrid methods must track both scales rather than reporting a single undifferentiated error.

Approximate linear algebra in statistics

Remark. Large-scale regression, Gaussian processes, inverse problems, and Bayesian computation depend on approximate linear algebra. Preconditioning and numerical rank can affect not only speed but also the effective statistical model being fit.

Boundary with optimization

Remark. Optimization asks how to minimize or maximize an objective. Numerical analysis asks whether the finite computation accurately represents the mathematical problem. In statistical work the two meet, but they answer different questions.

Numerically stable likelihood calculations

Products of small densities underflow; use log likelihoods. For \(a_i\in\mathbb R\), \[ \log\sum_i e^{a_i} =m+\log\sum_i e^{a_i-m}, \qquad m=\max_i a_i. \] The shifted exponents lie in \((0,1]\), preventing overflow. Related functions \(\log(1+x)\) and \(e^x-1\) should use log1p and expm1 near zero to avoid cancellation.

Covariance and positive semidefiniteness

The one-pass identity \[ \sum x_i^2-n\bar x^2 \] can catastrophically cancel. Welford's recurrence updates \[ \delta_n=x_n-\bar x_{n-1},\qquad \bar x_n=\bar x_{n-1}+\delta_n/n, \] \[ M_{2,n}=M_{2,n-1} +\delta_n(x_n-\bar x_n). \] It is stable and supports parallel pairwise combination. For covariance matrices, square-root factorizations preserve positive semidefiniteness better than subtractive formulas.

Optimization error versus statistical error

If an estimator minimizes empirical criterion \(L_n\) but an algorithm returns \(\widetilde\theta\) with \[ L_n(\widetilde\theta)-\inf_\theta L_n(\theta)\le\varepsilon_{\mathrm opt}, \] strong convexity \(m\) gives \[ \|\widetilde\theta-\widehat\theta\| \le\sqrt{2\varepsilon_{\mathrm opt}/m}. \] To preserve a root-\(n\) statistical limit, it often suffices that this parameter error be \(o_p(n^{-1/2})\), or that the score residual be \(o_p(n^{-1/2})\) under Hessian stability. Solving far beyond statistical precision wastes computation.

Linear algebra in regression

Normal equations square the condition number of a design matrix. QR gives stable ordinary least squares; SVD exposes weakly identified directions; pivoted QR diagnoses rank; iterative solvers need preconditioning. Standard errors inherit numerical error through inverses or factor solves, so a coefficient estimate without a stable covariance computation can still be misleading.

Monte Carlo error and pseudorandomness

Theorem. For iid samples and \(E f(X)^2<\infty\), \[ \widehat I_N=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^Nf(X_i) \] has \[ E(\widehat I_N-I)^2=\frac{\operatorname{Var}(f(X))}{N}. \]

Proof. Unbiasedness is linearity of expectation; independence makes variances add: \[ \operatorname{Var}(\widehat I_N) =N^{-2}N\operatorname{Var}(f(X)). \]

Markov chain Monte Carlo replaces variance by long-run variance and effective sample size. Quasi-Monte Carlo can approach \(N^{-1}\) error for smooth, low-effective-dimensional integrands. Rounding error is usually smaller than sampling error but can matter in enormous reductions or rare-event weights.

Randomized numerical linear algebra

Leverage-score sampling and random projections approximate regression, low-rank structure, and matrix products. A subspace embedding \(S\) satisfies \[ (1-\varepsilon)\|Ax\|^2 \le\|SAx\|^2 \le(1+\varepsilon)\|Ax\|^2 \] for every \(x\). Solving the sketched least-squares problem then gives a controlled residual approximation. Probability of numerical success and floating-point stability are separate layers and both require analysis.

Reproducibility

Random seeds do not ensure bitwise reproducibility when parallel reductions, hardware kernels, or nondeterministic scheduling change operation order. Scientific reproducibility should record data, software, tolerances, stopping rules, precision, and diagnostics; numerical uncertainty should be compared with sampling and model uncertainty rather than silently ignored.
