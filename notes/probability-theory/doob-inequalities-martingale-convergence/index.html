Maximal inequalities convert control at a terminal time into control of an entire path. Upcrossing inequalities convert one-sided expectation bounds into almost-sure convergence. These two mechanisms are the martingale counterparts of Kolmogorov's maximal inequality and compactness by oscillation control.

Weak maximal inequality

Definition. For a process \(X\), write \[ X_n^*=\max_{0\le k\le n}|X_k|. \] For a one-sided submartingale inequality, write \(\overline X_n=\max_{0\le k\le n}X_k\).

Theorem (Doob weak maximal inequality). If \(X_0,\ldots,X_n\) is a nonnegative submartingale, then, for every \(\lambda>0\), \[ \lambda P(\overline X_n\ge\lambda) \le \mathbb E[X_n1_{\{\overline X_n\ge\lambda\}}] \le\mathbb EX_n. \]

Proof. Partition the first-crossing event into \[ A_k=\{X_0<\lambda,\ldots,X_{k-1}<\lambda,\ X_k\ge\lambda\}, \qquad0\le k\le n. \] The \(A_k\) are disjoint, \(A_k\in\mathcal F_k\), and their union is \(\{\overline X_n\ge\lambda\}\). Since a submartingale satisfies \(X_k\le\mathbb E[X_n\mid\mathcal F_k]\), \[ \lambda P(A_k) \le\mathbb E[X_k1_{A_k}] \le\mathbb E[X_n1_{A_k}]. \] Sum over \(k\). Nonnegativity gives the final inequality.

Corollary. If \(M\) is a martingale, then \[ \lambda P(M_n^*\ge\lambda) \le\mathbb E[|M_n|1_{\{M_n^*\ge\lambda\}}], \] because \(|M_k|\) is a nonnegative submartingale.

The \(L^p\) maximal inequality

Theorem (Doob \(L^p\) inequality). If \(p>1\), \(q=p/(p-1)\), and \(M_n\in L^p\), then \[ \|M_n^*\|_p\le q\|M_n\|_p. \] More generally, the same bound holds for the positive maximum of a nonnegative submartingale.

Proof. For \(X=\max_{k\le n}X_k\) and nonnegative submartingale \(X_k\), the layer-cake formula and the weak inequality give \[ \begin{aligned} \mathbb EX^p &=p\int_0^\infty\lambda^{p-1}P(X\ge\lambda)\,\mathrm{d}\lambda\\ &\le p\int_0^\infty\lambda^{p-2} \mathbb E[X_n1_{\{X\ge\lambda\}}]\,\mathrm{d}\lambda\\ &=\frac{p}{p-1}\mathbb E[X_nX^{p-1}]. \end{aligned} \] Holder's inequality bounds the last expression by \(q\|X_n\|_p\|X\|_p^{p-1}\). Divide by \(\|X\|_p^{p-1}\). Apply the result to \(|M_k|\).

Corollary (\(L^p\) martingale convergence). If \(p>1\) and \(\sup_n\mathbb E|M_n|^p<\infty\), then there is \(M_\infty\in L^p\) such that \[ M_n\to M_\infty \quad\text{almost surely and in }L^p. \]

Proof. The submartingale convergence theorem below gives almost-sure convergence because \(\sup_n\mathbb E|M_n|<\infty\). Fatou gives \(M_\infty\in L^p\). For fixed \(m\), the process \(M_{m+k}-M_m\) is a martingale, so Doob's inequality and conditional expectation representation yield uniform \(L^p\) control of tails. Equivalently, \(L^p\)-boundedness with \(p>1\) implies uniform integrability of \(|M_n|^p\); almost-sure convergence plus Vitali then gives \(\mathbb E|M_n-M_\infty|^p\to0\).
