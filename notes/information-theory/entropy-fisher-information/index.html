Entropy and Fisher information meet along Gaussian smoothing. This link explains why central-limit phenomena and information inequalities often share the same proofs.

Fisher information of a density

Definition. For a smooth density \(f\) on \(\mathbb R^d\), the Fisher information for translation is \[ J(X)=\int \|\nabla\log f(x)\|^2 f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x. \]

de Bruijn identity

Theorem. Let \(Z\sim N(0,I_d)\) be independent of \(X\). Then, under regularity conditions, \[ \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}h(X+\sqrt t Z)=\frac12 J(X+\sqrt t Z). \]

Stam inequality

Corollary. For independent random vectors, \[ \frac1{J(X+Y)}\ge \frac1{J(X)}+\frac1{J(Y)} \] in the scalar form, with suitable matrix versions in higher dimension. This is the Fisher-information analogue of EPI.

Statistical meaning

Remark. Fisher information controls local parametric sensitivity, while entropy controls global spread. Gaussian convolution reveals a differential relation between these two notions, giving a clean bridge to Cramér-Rao and asymptotic normality themes in the statistics notes.

Scores and Fisher information for location

Definition. For a smooth density \(f\) on \(\mathbb R^d\), its score is \[ \rho_X(x)=\nabla\log f(x), \] and its translation Fisher information is \[ J(X)=\mathbb E\|\rho_X(X)\|_2^2. \]

Proposition. Under boundary regularity, \[ \mathbb E\rho_X(X)=0. \]

Proof. \[ \mathbb E\rho_X(X)= \int\nabla\log f(x)f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x =\int\nabla f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x=0 \] when boundary terms vanish.

de Bruijn's identity

Theorem. Let \(Z\sim N_d(0,I)\) be independent and \(X_t=X+\sqrt t\,Z\). Then \[ \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}h(X_t) =\frac12J(X_t),\qquad t>0, \] under standard integrability conditions.

Proof. The density \(f_t\) solves the heat equation \(\partial_tf_t=\tfrac12\Delta f_t\). Differentiate entropy: \[ \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}h(f_t) =-\int(1+\log f_t)\partial_tf_t\,\mathrm{d}x =-\frac12\int\log f_t\,\Delta f_t\,\mathrm{d}x. \] Integration by parts gives \[ \frac12\int\frac{\|\nabla f_t\|^2}{f_t}\,\mathrm{d}x =\frac12J(X_t). \]

Entropy increases under Gaussian smoothing

Corollary. \(t\mapsto h(X+\sqrt tZ)\) is nondecreasing, strictly unless the score vanishes in the relevant sense.

Gaussian convolution destroys fine-scale structure and increases uncertainty. Fisher information is the instantaneous rate of entropy increase along this heat flow.

Fisher-information inequality

Theorem. For independent scalar variables, \[ J(X+Y)\le \frac{J(X)J(Y)}{J(X)+J(Y)}. \]

This is equivalent to Stam's reciprocal inequality. It says convolution decreases location information, paralleling data processing.

Cramér-Rao from score geometry

Theorem. If \(X\) is centered with variance \(\sigma^2\), then \[ J(X)\ge\frac1{\sigma^2}, \] with equality exactly for a Gaussian law under regularity.

Proof. Integration by parts gives \[ \mathbb E[X\rho_X(X)]=-1. \] Cauchy-Schwarz yields \(1\le\mathbb EX^2\,\mathbb E\rho_X(X)^2 =\sigma^2J(X)\). Equality requires \(\rho_X(X)=-X/\sigma^2\), whose differential equation gives the Gaussian density.

Relative Fisher information and Gaussian convergence

Definition. Relative Fisher information of \(X\) to a smooth reference \(G\) is \[ J(X\|G)= \mathbb E\|\nabla\log f_X(X)- \nabla\log f_G(X)\|^2. \]

For standard Gaussian \(G\), the relative score is \(\rho_X(X)+X\). Along Ornstein-Uhlenbeck flow, relative entropy to Gaussian decreases, and its derivative is minus relative Fisher information. Log-Sobolev inequalities integrate this derivative to control KL by Fisher information.

I-MMSE identity

Theorem. Let \(Y_\gamma=\sqrt\gamma X+Z\), \(Z\sim N(0,1)\) independent, and \(\mathbb EX^2<\infty\). Then \[ \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}\gamma}I(X;Y_\gamma) =\frac12\operatorname{mmse}(X\mid Y_\gamma), \] where \[ \operatorname{mmse}(X\mid Y)= \mathbb E\{X-\mathbb E(X\mid Y)\}^2. \]

Proof outline. Write \(I(X;Y_\gamma)=h(Y_\gamma)-h(Z)\). Rescale \(Y_\gamma=\sqrt\gamma(X+\gamma^{-1/2}Z)\) and apply de Bruijn's identity. The output score satisfies Tweedie's identity \[ \rho_{Y_\gamma}(y)= \sqrt\gamma\,\mathbb E(X\mid Y_\gamma=y)-y. \] Substitution and variance decomposition reduce the derivative to half the posterior mean-squared error.

Mutual information as integrated estimation error

Corollary. \[ I(X;\sqrt\gamma X+Z) =\frac12\int_0^\gamma \operatorname{mmse}(X\mid\sqrt sX+Z)\,\mathrm{d}s. \]

This gives a precise interface between information and Bayesian estimation: mutual information accumulated along signal-to-noise ratio equals integrated posterior uncertainty.

Bayesian Fisher identities

For \(Y=X+\sigma Z\), the score of the marginal density is \[ \nabla\log p_Y(y)= \frac{\mathbb E(X\mid Y=y)-y}{\sigma^2}. \] Thus posterior mean denoising and score estimation are equivalent. This identity underlies empirical Bayes, score matching, and diffusion-model denoisers.
